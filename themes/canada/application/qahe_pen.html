International Association for Quality Assurance in Higher Education (QAHE)

sed to welcome Prof. Eze C. Nwauba for joining QAHE as the Country Director (Nigeria)

  • May 28, 2022
  • admin

We are pleased to welcome Prof. Eze C. Nwauba for joining International Association for Quality Assurance in Higher Education (QAHE) as the Country Director (Nigeria). Prof. Eze C. Nwauba is a Professor of Intrapreneurship, and Adjunct Faculty at Prowess University, USA; he is an Adjunct Professor of Public Administration at IBLT University, Togo, and a Visiting Professor at ESTG University Republic of Benin. Nwauba is the Director, School of Part-time Studies Legacy University Okija, Nigeria, and the Registrar; Institute of Arts Management & Professional Studies, Nigeria. He holds a Ph.D. in Public Administration from Ambrose Alli University (AAU-Nigeria). Over the last 15 years, Nwauba has taught on a wide range of open and tailored programmes at Madonna University, Okija, Imo State University (ICEP), and other academic institutions including Tansian University (School of Economics) Umunya, Nigeria, ISTAG Benin University, Republic of Benin (Dean Management Faculty), Courage University, Republic of Benin, and The Nobel International Business School, Accra-Ghana. He was also an Adjunct Faculty, Lorma Colleges, San Fernando City, La Union, Philippines.

  • Nwauba is a Fellow of Chartered Entrepreneur Management of Nigeria, Chartered Institute of Arts Management & Professionals, USA, African Professional Managers, Chartered Institute of Mentoring and Coaching, Nigeria, and Institute of Business Education. Besides, he is a Member Editorial Board of Pan-African Journal of Humanities and Social Sciences (PAJHSS); Editorial Consultant of International Journal of The Age of Reasoning (IJAR); Managing Editor of International Journal of Arts Management & Professional Studies (IJAMPS); Member Reviewer Committee of Emirate Association of Arts Management Professionals (EAAMP), UAE; Member (DEAC Community) of Distance Education Accrediting Commission (DEAC), Washington; and a Reviewer of International Journal of Scientific Research and Management (IJSRM). Furthermore, Prof. Eze Nwauba has published many articles in leading academic journals and has also co-authored several books. He has a strong research interest in Governance, Human Resource Management, Organizational Leadership and Management, Public Policy, and Entrepreneurship, among others.
  • We believe the participation of Prof. Eze C. Nwauba would greatly benefit the Association in many levels.
