Once you have been accepted, the postsecondary institution will send you a letter of admission. This document will provide important details on the next steps required. It may also indicate whether some transfer credits were awarded.

It will be entirely up to you to decide whether to accept or refuse this offer. If you have submitted an application to more than one institution, you may have a number of options available for consideration.

If you need a study permit or visitor's visa to enter Canada, the letter of admission will be required by the immigration authorities in order to process your immigration application.
