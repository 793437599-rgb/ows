{:lang('ABOUT WSE')}

{:lang('GOALS')}

{:lang('For a number of years informal groups of international credential evaluators around the world have been discussing the need for a new professional association. WSE was established to meet the unique goals of the profession of international credential evaluation')}.

{:lang('The primary goals of the Association are to')}:

  • {:lang('Define and promote the profession')}
  • {:lang('Develop and promote common standards')}
  • {:lang('Advocate for the profession')}
  • {:lang('Conduct research and disseminate information that supports the profession')}
  • {:lang('Identify and develop theories, methodologies and best practices used in international credential evaluation')}
  • {:lang('Obtain appropriate official recognition for the association')}
  • {:lang('Promote cooperation with other groups involved in international education')}
  • {:lang('Identify and promote professional development, training, and other educational opportunities')}

{:lang('OPERATIONAL PRINCIPLES')}

{:lang('The operational principles of the association are:')}

  • {:lang('Collaboration')}
  • {:lang('Transparency')}
  • {:lang('Inclusiveness')}
  • {:lang('Volunteerism')}
  • {:lang('International orientation')}

{:lang('STANDARDS DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLES')}

  • {:lang('Openness – the opportunity for involvement by all parties known to be affected by the particular standards development activity')}
  • {:lang('Balance – balancing interests so that standards development activities are not dominated by any single group of interested parties')}
  • {:lang('Transparency – readily available access to essential information regarding proposed and final standards')}
  • {:lang('Consensus – substantial agreement reached on all material points after the consideration of all views and objections')}
  • {:lang('Process – including the right to express a position, to have it considered, and to appeal an adverse decision')}
