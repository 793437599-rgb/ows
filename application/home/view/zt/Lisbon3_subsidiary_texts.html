Since 1999, the Lisbon Recognition Convention Committee has adopted a series of subsidiary texts to support the implementation of the convention.

These include:

  1. 1. Recommendation on Recognition of Qualifications Held by Refugees, Displaced Persons and Persons in a Refugee-like Situation
  2. 2. Revised Recommendation on the Recognition of Joint Degrees;
  3. 3.Recommendation on the Use of Qualifications Frameworks in the Recognition of Foreign Qualifications;
  4. 4. Revised Recommendation on Criteria and Procedures for the Assessment of Foreign Qualifications and Explanatory Memorandum;
  5. 5. Revised Code of Good Practice in the Provision of Transnational Education;
  6. 6. Diploma Supplement;
