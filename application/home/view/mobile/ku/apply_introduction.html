CA UNIVERSITY-Foreign Digital Transmission Service

Function Description
Chinese students could send electronic verification reports (China Higher Education Student Record, China Higher Education Qualification Certificate, China Higher Education Student’s Academic Transcript, China National College Entrance Examination Results, China Secondary Education Qualification Certificate, China Secondary Education Student’s Academic Transcript and China High School Academic Proficiency Test Results) to the foreign universities via the CA UNIVERSITY-foreign transmission service. Click “How to apply” link on the corresponding CA UNIVERSITY-foreign portal page to view the details.
Postsecondary
Education
Includes the graduate, undergraduate and junior college levels

Chinese students

Use “Name” and “ID Number” to register your CHSI Archive, then check your student record (for those who have not completed their studies) or higher education qualification certificate (for those who have already graduated) information. If your student record or qualification certificate information cannot be found in your “CHSI Archive”, please contact the related management department in your school to check your name and personal identification number. Then use “Name” + “Identification Number” to conduct a re-registration to check the existence of your educational information.

The personal information filled on the foreign school’s application websites should be consistent with that in your “CHSI Archive”.

Please use the same personal identification number as filled in your CHSI Archive when applying for “Verification Report of China Higher Education Qualification Certificate”, “Verification Report of China Higher Education Student’s Academic Transcript” and other verification reports.

Students from other countries

Please contact the related management department in your school to confirm the personal identification number used to register your student record and higher education qualification. Then use the same identification number to apply for the “Verification Report of China Higher Education Qualification Certificate”, “Verification Report of China Higher Education Student’s Academic Transcript” and other verification reports as filled on the foreign school’s application websites.

Secondary
Education
Includes the high-school and secondary vocational education levels

The personal identification number used on the foreign school’s application websites, the CHSI Archive registration, the application for “Verification Report of China National College Entrance Examination (Gaokao) Scores”, “Verification Report of China Secondary Education Qualification Certificate”, “Verification Report of China Secondary Education Student’s Academic Transcript”, “Verification Report of China High School Academic Proficiency Test (Huikao) Results” should be the same.
