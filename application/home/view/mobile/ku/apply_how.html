（Flowchart of Cooperative Application）

Firstly complete the online college application according to CollegeNET’s requirement. Then follow the instructions below to apply for verification report(s) and submit to U.S. universities through the CA UNIVERSITY—CollegeNET Portal Service.

  1. STEP 1: REGISTER a CA UNIVERSITY ACCOUNT
  2. STEP 2: APPLY FOR YOUR REPORT(S)
    1. 1.Log in to the CA UNIVERSITY Website, choose “International Cooperative Application”;
    2. 2.Select translation types:
      1. Online Verification Report for Higher Education Student Record;
      2. Online Verification Report for Higher Education Qualification;
      3. Verification Report of China Higher Education Qualification Certificate;
      4. China Higher Education Degree Certificate Information (Translation).
    3. Other Verification Reports
      1. Go to Online Application Platform to apply for required verification report(s);
      2. For Chinese degree verification please go to CDGDC.
  3. STEP 3: DELIVER YOUR REPORT(S)
    1. Log in to CA UNIVERSITY Archive and choose "International Cooperative Application", click “Send Report(s)”;
    2. Select country and university from drop-down list, and enter the requested personal information;
    3. Select report(s) that you wish to send, confirm and send.
